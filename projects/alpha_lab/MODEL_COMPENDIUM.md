4. this compendium for cross-model memory and reusable lessons.

## Required interpretation

- `NEGATIVE` means an outcome-bearing test failed its frozen economic or
  statistical criteria.
- `DATA_BLOCKED` means the model was not tested. It is a lesson about research
  feasibility, not evidence against the investment mechanism.
- `ACTIVE_RESEARCH` means no terminal result exists.
- Registry status records lifecycle posture; it is not proof of alpha.
- A changed feature, universe, horizon, benchmark, or construction rule is a
  child experiment, never a rewrite of an earlier result.

## Negative-result lessons

### Do not search parameter neighborhoods after an economic failure

Residual momentum, issuer seasonality, and reversal failed under their frozen
constructions. A new lookback or threshold is not a new idea. Revisit only when
the economic mechanism, information source, or implementation constraint is
materially different and receives a new hypothesis identity.

### Costs and capacity answer different questions

All three evaluated negative models supported approximately `$1M` capacity.
Capacity did not make them profitable. Reversal additionally failed because
turnover costs amplified an already weak result. Future short-horizon ideas
must establish a plausible gross edge and an implementable turnover path before
consuming a formal trial.

### Relative superiority is not positive alpha

The seasonality signal exceeded its placebo while both were economically
negative. Future research must require positive net economics against an
investable baseline, not merely a ranking among losing variants.

### Regime slices cannot rescue a failed unconditional test

Secondary regime diagnostics are descriptive. A regime-specific rescue claim
requires its own mechanism, frozen interaction hypothesis, and untouched
evidence; it cannot be selected after seeing a negative aggregate result.

### Data feasibility is part of idea quality

Nine registered experiments stopped before returns. Before freezing a new
idea, identify the exact timestamped inputs and a credible acquisition path.
Prefer ideas using already certified data unless the expected information gain
justifies an explicit owner-support request.

## Positive and promoted-result lessons

No Alpha Lab experiment currently has an `EVIDENCE_READY_FOR_OWNER_REVIEW`
positive verdict. No owner `PURSUE`, `PARK`, or `KILL` model decision is recorded
in `DECISION_LOG.md` beyond the decision to pursue the Alpha Lab project itself.
Current Paper and Shadow registry states predate this experiment series and
must not be presented as Alpha Lab-validated alpha.

When a positive result or lifecycle decision occurs, add it here with the same
specificity required for a negative result: surviving mechanism, factor and
cost boundary, capacity, failure conditions, next permitted state, and evidence
hashes.

## Next-cycle research constraints

Before freezing a new model, answer:

1. Is its mechanism distinct from every row above?
2. Does it avoid a known failed construction rather than merely rename it?
3. Are the event, feature, universe, and return timestamps available?
4. Can a simple investable baseline falsify it cheaply?
5. What exact result would add new knowledge even if negative?

Use `RESEARCH_IDEA_GENERATION_WORKFLOW.md` to answer these questions before
generating candidates. Record the resulting opportunity-map lineage in the
hypothesis's mandatory **Memory-derived opportunity map and prior-model
review** section. For `HYP-2026-015` onward, missing review evidence blocks
freeze.

The frozen `HYP-2026-015` industry earnings-information diffusion idea is
potentially informative because it replaces unavailable analyst revisions and
supplier graphs with observable, timestamped reporter reactions. Its authorized
no-return gate stopped `BLOCKED_DATA` on one missing Item 2.02 original. Owner-
approved Addendum 001 now permits a bounded materiality rerun and freezes the
previously ambiguous evaluator rules. It remains `UNPROVEN`; the rerun must
still show that peer returns are not ordinary industry or raw momentum.
