# Purpose

The core execution, attribution, governance, and promotion framework is now sufficiently mature to support multiple independent strategy families.

The primary objective of the next phase is no longer operational reliability. The objective is to identify and validate multiple independent sources of alpha which can eventually compete for capital allocation within the Caerus platform.

Future portfolio construction should avoid concentration in highly correlated strategies and instead seek diversification across distinct return drivers.

This document is strategic context only. For authoritative FR status and the operational FR queue, use `fr_registry.md` and `fr_active_backlog.md`.

# Strategic Direction

Current analysis suggests Polaris, Orion, and Lyra exhibit significant behavioral overlap.

While this overlap provides additional observations regarding robustness, it does not materially expand the platform's opportunity set.

Future development should prioritize strategies with differentiated alpha sources.

Primary momentum and quality-driven portfolio intended to serve as the long-term benchmark strategy.

Status: Active

Role: Secondary Core Portfolio

Alternative factor expression intended to challenge Polaris.

## Phoenix

Role: Crisis Reversal Strategy

Objective:

Systematically identify and exploit temporary market overreactions.

Characteristics:

- Panic selling
- Sharp drawdowns
- High-volume dislocations
- Recovery candidates
- Mean reversion opportunities

Expected Holding Period:

5-20 trading days

Expected Behavior:

Low correlation to momentum strategies.

Priority:

HIGH

---

## Cygnus

Role: Earnings Drift Strategy

Objective:

Capture post-earnings announcement drift and estimate revision persistence.

Characteristics:

- Earnings beats
- Upward revisions
- Positive guidance
- Momentum continuation following catalyst events

Expected Holding Period:

10-60 trading days

Expected Behavior:

Moderate correlation to momentum but driven by distinct catalyst mechanics.

Priority:

HIGH

---

## Cassiopeia

Role: Event-Driven Strategy

Objective:

Capture returns generated by corporate and market catalysts.

Potential Signals:

- Analyst upgrades
- Analyst downgrades
- CEO transitions
- Spin-offs
- M&A activity
- Index additions/removals
- Activist involvement

Expected Holding Period:

Variable

Expected Behavior:

Low-to-moderate correlation with core momentum strategies.

Priority:

MEDIUM

---

## Argo

Role: Regime Overlay

Objective:

Determine market environment and allocate capital across strategies.

Potential Inputs:

- VIX regime
- Realized volatility
- Breadth indicators
- Trend strength
- Credit conditions
- Market concentration

Potential Outputs:

- Capital allocation adjustments
- Strategy weighting recommendations
- Risk budget adjustments

Important:

Argo is not initially expected to generate securities.

Argo should first be developed as a research and allocation layer.

Priority:

MEDIUM

# Success Definition

The platform should evolve from:

- Polaris
- Orion
- Lyra

to:

- Polaris
- Orion
- Phoenix
- Cygnus
- Cassiopeia

with Argo serving as a portfolio allocation layer.

Long-term success is defined not by identifying a single winning strategy, but by creating a diversified collection of independent alpha sources capable of competing for capital allocation within a unified governance framework.

The next phase of the Caerus program is focused on alpha discovery, strategy diversification, and evidence-based capital allocation.
