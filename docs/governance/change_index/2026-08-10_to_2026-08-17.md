## Executive synthesis

The week was not a series of unrelated patches. It was one compressed migration
through five layers:

1. Orion PAPER evidence and reconciliation were allowed to use the immediately
   preceding XNYS session without losing immutable lineage.
2. Target attainment moved to a governed whole-share, nearest-feasible model.
3. PAPER execution moved to immutable exact plans, stable broker identities,
   append-only WAL/recovery, explicit terminal outcomes, and economic proof.
4. Precompute moved to a schema-3 institutional portfolio operating model:
   session, sleeve decisions, account allocation, sealed target, exact orders,
   causal ownership, broker valuation, and daily audit.
5. The August 17 incident exposed one remaining contradiction: authorization
   could reconstruct full broker NAV while sizing from a lower synthetic
   planning balance. That path is now rejected before any WAL intent or broker
   submission.

## August 17 incident chain

The sealed target itself was correct: INTC, LRCX, MU, STX, and WDC at 19% each
with 5% cash. The 09:35 exact plan was not. Its immutable evidence contained two
different account bases:

- broker-authoritative NAV: **$11,822.55**;
- planning equity and capital ceiling: **$10,000.00**.

That lower basis produced an LRCX sell that a full-account replay does not
produce. At the same 09:35 holdings and prices, the valid nearest-feasible plan
was INTC +4, LRCX +1, and WDC +1, with no sells. Because prices moved before the
governed corrective epoch, the later correction used a different valid exact
delta. The final broker portfolio reconciled, but the same-day sell/buy churn
was operationally unacceptable.

The final control at `5c0a2cd` classifies the original plan as
`planning_equity_cap_reduces_authoritative_account_nav` before order intent and
classifies the corrective full-account plan as valid.
