# Operator Review Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow operationalizes daily telemetry consumption. It tells the operator
how to read the Daily Research Interpretation Packet without confusing advisory
research telemetry with execution or promotion logic.

## Daily Review Order

Review in this order:

1. Operational Trust Summary
2. Executive Summary
3. Strategy Comparison Summary
4. Exposure + Concentration Review
5. Regime Interpretation
6. Fragility Observations
7. Confidence + Freshness Caveats
8. Key Risks
9. Research Follow-Ups

Trust comes first. A strong return with weak evidence should not outrank a clear
confidence caveat.

## 5 Minute Quick Review

Use this when checking daily status quickly.

1. Confirm packet status is not `PARTIAL` due to missing core evidence.
2. Read the strategy rank leader and daily return context.
3. Check whether Orion or Lyra outperformance coincides with concentration
   flags.
4. Check whether regime fragility indicators fired.
5. Read the LOW confidence caveat for operational shadow NAV.

Escalate if:

- packet status is `PARTIAL`;
- expected shadow or research clarity artifacts are missing;
- Orion or Lyra lead with high concentration;
- freshness is unknown on core inputs;
- regime fragility indicators are present.

## 15 Minute Deep Review

Use this for research interpretation.

1. Compare Polaris, Orion, and Lyra daily returns.
2. Inspect top-three concentration and max position weight.
3. Review sector exposure and momentum sensitivity.
4. Check regime evidence and fragility indicators.
5. Compare today with prior packets if available.
6. Separate selection alpha hypotheses from concentration amplification.
7. Record follow-up questions for weekly synthesis.

## Weekly Synthesis Mode

Use this to convert daily observations into research conclusions.

1. Review packets across the week.
2. Identify repeated concentration or sector exposure.
3. Identify repeated regime dependency.
4. Track whether Orion or Lyra remain leaders after caveats.
5. Separate durable behavior from one-day amplification.
6. Prepare FR-028 and FR-029 questions without changing governance rules.

## Escalation Triggers

Escalate for operator review when:

- source artifacts are missing or freshness is unknown;
- broker and shadow surfaces are being compared without caveats;
- operational shadow NAV is described above LOW confidence before FR-028;
- a challenger outperforms while carrying high concentration;
- regime fragility persists across multiple packets;
- a packet appears to imply promotion readiness.

## Threshold Guidance

Initial review thresholds:

- top-three concentration at or above 60%: high concentration review;
- max position weight at or above 20%: high position concentration review;
- max sector exposure at or above 50%: sector concentration review;
- missing core packet input: partial confidence;
- unknown freshness on core packet input: do not treat the packet as complete.

These thresholds are interpretation aids only. They do not gate execution,
promotion, or portfolio changes.

## Confidence Interpretation

LOW confidence does not mean useless. It means the evidence can support research
questions but should not support authoritative performance claims.

MEDIUM or HIGH confidence must come from provenance, freshness, and lineage. It
must not come from strong returns alone.

## Review Notes

Daily packet review should produce questions, not trades.

Examples:

- Did Orion outperform because of stock selection or top-three concentration?
- Is Lyra more regime-fragile than Polaris?
- Is the winning strategy concentrated in Information Technology?
- Are packet inputs fresh enough to trust today's interpretation?
- Which findings should be carried into the weekly research synthesis?
